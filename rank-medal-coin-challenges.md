# Rank, Medal, and Coin Definitions

## Core Concepts
- **Rank:** Tiered standing that reflects cumulative performance across activities and challenges. Ranks advance by earning experience from completed activities, challenge completions, and streaks.
- **Medals:** Discrete achievement badges tied to milestones (distance, elevation, speed, consistency) and special events. Medals can unlock cosmetic flair on the dashboard and contribute to rank progression.
- **Coins:** In-app currency awarded for activities, streaks, and challenge completions. Coins fund perks such as entry into special challenges, cosmetic upgrades, and leaderboard boosts; they are also surfaced in the wallet view.

## Monthly Challenges (10)
Each month tracks completion percentage and awards coins and a medal on completion. Suggested defaults: 100 coins + 1 medal per completed challenge, with 25 bonus coins for finishing before day 20.

1. **January — Winter Base Builder:** Log 120 km running and 400 km cycling; maintain at least one activity per week.
2. **February — Elevation Focus:** Accumulate 3,000 m of climbing across any discipline and complete two hill repeat sessions.
3. **March — Consistency Sprint:** Complete 16 activities (min 20 minutes each) with no more than two consecutive rest days.
4. **April — Long Ride Month:** Ride a total of 600 km, including one ride of 120 km or longer.
5. **May — Mixed Modalities:** Complete 8 runs, 6 rides, and 2 cross-training sessions (yoga/strength) tracked in-app.
6. **June — Speed Tune-Up:** Record three runs with sub-5:30/km pace and two rides with 30 km/h average speed over 20 km.
7. **July — Heat Resilience:** Finish 10 outdoor activities logged between 10am–5pm local time and hydrate log on 8 of them.
8. **August — Streak Saver:** Maintain a 10-day active streak (any activity counts) and total 12 hours of activity time.
9. **September — Endurance Block:** Complete two back-to-back long days (90+ minutes each) and hit 15 total hours.
10. **October — Event Ready:** Finish one official race/ride upload and total 800 TSS-equivalent points (or provided training load metric).

## Special Challenges (5)
Special challenges can be limited-time or premium-access; award 250 coins + 2 medals upon completion unless otherwise noted.

1. **World Climb Weekend:** 48-hour window to climb 2,500 m; partials grant scaled coins but no medals.
2. **Night Owl:** Log three activities starting after 9pm local time within a rolling 10-day window.
3. **Team Relay:** Join a team of up to four; collectively log 300 km in 7 days. Requires 50-coin entry fee.
4. **Recovery Discipline:** Seven-day block with at least four recovery sessions (HRZ1/2) and two mobility uploads.
5. **Personal Best Push:** Beat your best 5k run pace or 20-minute cycling power; verify with previous in-app PR data.
