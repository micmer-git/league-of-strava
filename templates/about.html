About League of Strava

Welcome to the League of Strava, a project curated by Micmer Michele Merelli. This platform empowers users to log their activities and earn unique achievements and medals based on their performance. Whether you're a runner, cyclist, or hiker, our system celebrates your milestones with engaging badges and rewards.

🔝 Ranking System Overview

The **Ranking System** is designed to foster a competitive yet supportive community. Users are ranked based on various metrics such as total points accumulated through achievements and medals. Higher ranks unlock exclusive features and badges, motivating users to stay active and strive for excellence.

💰 Coins System

The **Coins System** rewards users with virtual coins as they unlock achievements and medals. These coins can be utilized in various ways to enhance your experience on the platform:

Earning Coins

  • Achievements: Earn 10 coins for each achievement unlocked.
  • Medals: Earn 5 coins for each medal earned.

Using Coins

  • Unlock Exclusive Features: Redeem coins to access premium features such as advanced analytics, personalized training plans, and more.
  • Customize Your Profile: Use coins to personalize your profile with unique themes, badges, and avatars.
  • Participate in Special Events: Enter exclusive competitions and events that require a certain number of coins to join.

Earn coins effortlessly by achieving your goals and stay motivated to reach new heights!

🏆 Achievements

**Achievements** are milestones that recognize your dedication and performance. They are automatically unlocked as you meet specific criteria in your logged activities. Each achievement is represented with a unique emoji, making your progress visually appealing.

Core Achievements

  • Longest Streak 🔥: Maintain the longest consecutive days with logged activities. Unlock higher levels as your streak grows.
  • Distance Badges 💯🔱⚜️: Complete activities covering significant distances:
    • 100 km 💯
    • 200 km 🔱
    • 300 km ⚜️
  • Duration Badges ⌛⏱️🌇: Engage in long-duration activities:
    • 3 Hours ⌛
    • 6 Hours ⏱️
    • 12 Hours 🌇
  • Weekly Badges 💰🧈💎: Achieve weekly activity goals:
    • 5 Hours Week 💰
    • 10 Hours Week 🧈
    • 20 Hours Week 💎
  • Consistency Champion 🔁: Log activities every day for an entire month.
  • Daily kcal Burner 🔥: Burn over 2,000 kcal in total activities.

Advanced Achievements

  • Everesting Ascent 🏔️: Complete an activity with an elevation gain equivalent to Mount Everest (~8,848m).
  • 2000m Ascent ⬆️: Log an activity with a total elevation gain of at least 2,000 meters.
  • Half Everesting 🌄: Achieve an elevation gain of approximately half of Everest's height (~4,424m).
  • 2000 km Month 📅: Cover a total distance of 2,000 kilometers within a single month.
  • 10,000 km Year 🌍: Accumulate a total distance of 10,000 kilometers over the course of a year.
  • 100,000 Elevation Year 🧗: Reach a total elevation gain of 100,000 meters in a year.
  • 150,000 Elevation Year 🚀: Achieve a yearly elevation gain of 150,000 meters.
  • 200,000 Elevation Year 🏔️🏔️: The ultimate achievement for a yearly elevation gain of 200,000 meters.
  • Marathon Master 🇬🇷: Successfully complete a marathon (42.195 kilometers).
  • Half Marathon Master 👟: Complete a half marathon (21.0975 kilometers).
  • Climbing King 🧗‍♂️: Accumulate a total elevation gain of over 1,000 meters.
  • Speedster 🏎️: Maintain an average speed exceeding 30 km/h in an activity.
🥇 Medals

**Medals** are special recognitions for specific challenges and exceptional performances. Unlike achievements, medals often require a combination of distance, elevation gain, and other activity-specific criteria.

Core Medals

  • New Year Run 🎉: Log activities on January 1st.
  • Christmas Run 🎄: Log activities on December 25th.
  • Valentine's Day ❤️: Log activities on February 14th.
  • Easter 🐣: Log activities on Easter Sunday (date varies yearly).
  • Halloween 🎃: Log activities on October 31st.
  • Thanksgiving 🦃: Log activities on Thanksgiving Day (fourth Thursday of November in the US).
  • Diwali 🪔: Log activities on Diwali (date varies yearly).
  • Hanukkah 🕎: Log activities during Hanukkah (date varies yearly).
  • Chinese New Year 🐉: Log activities on Chinese New Year (date varies yearly).
  • International Workers' Day ✊: Log activities on May 1st.

Special Medals

  • 20 km Challenge 🏅: Log an activity covering 20 kilometers or more.
  • Steep Climber 🧗‍♀️: Complete an activity with an elevation gain exceeding 3,000 meters and a distance less than 100 kilometers.
  • Coppa Coppi Protector 🥩: Achieve an elevation gain of over 2,000 meters in an activity shorter than 100 kilometers.
